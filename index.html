Your privacy matters

🔒
Locked chats

This demo can hide a chat behind a local passcode prompt. It does not encrypt the message database.

🛡️
No AI in LOOPA chats

This starter app has no AI integration and does not send messages to an AI service.

📱
Local demo storage

Your demo posts and messages stay in this browser's local storage. They do not sync to other devices or friends.

This starter version is not a secure messaging service. Real private chat requires a backend, tested end-to-end encryption, secure key handling, and authentication before it should be used with friends.

LOOPA principle

Your conversations belong to the people in them. No AI chat integration is included in this version.
